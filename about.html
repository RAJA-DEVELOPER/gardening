Our Roots

Born from a Greenhouse Dream

In 2011, founders Elena and Marco left their corporate careers to pursue something truly meaningful. They converted a 1930s greenhouse in Tuscany into Botanica's first home — a place where rare plants, handmade ceramics, and sustainable growing met under one glass roof.

What began as a weekend market stall grew into a beloved destination for design enthusiasts, landscape architects, and everyday plant lovers. Today, Botanica serves over 38 countries with the same passion and attention to detail as that very first stall.

Every seed we sell, every pot we craft, and every guide we write carries that original spirit: a deep reverence for the natural world and a commitment to helping you cultivate a more beautiful, greener life.

"Plants are not just decoration — they are living companions that clean our air, calm our minds, and connect us to something ancient and essential."

— Elena Romano, Co-Founder

Our Journey

15 Years of Growing

From a single greenhouse to a global botanical brand — here's how we grew.

2011

The Greenhouse Dream

Elena and Marco transform a crumbling 1930s Tuscan greenhouse into Botanica's first home. First Saturday market stall draws 200 visitors.

2014

First Online Store

Botanica.com launches and ships its first 500 plant orders across Europe within the first month.

2017

Organic Certification

All soil blends and fertilizers achieve USDA Organic certification. The sustainable packaging programme launches.

2021

10,000 Customers

A decade of growth sees Botanica reach 10,000 loyal customers across 25 countries. First B Corp certification awarded.

2026

Global Botanical Studio

Today, Botanica serves 38 countries, hosts 200+ workshop events per year, and remains passionately family-run.
